FAQ

How do I order?

Tap + on anything in the menu to add it to your cart, set quantities, then hit Checkout. Choose shipping or pickup, fill in your info, pay and submit.

Where’s my order?

Tap the button below, enter your order number (or payment code) and email, and tap Send. You’ll get an email back with your order status and tracking link, usually within the hour. Tracking is also emailed to you automatically the moment your order ships.

What’s a COA?

A Certificate of Analysis is a report from an independent lab that confirms what’s in the vial and how pure it is. COAs are available upon request. Just email me or ask in the Discord.

What’s a blend?

Two or more peptides combined in one vial, like Wolverine (BPC-157 + TB-500). Same compounds, more convenient.

What does “research use only” mean?

These compounds are sold for laboratory research. They are not approved medications, not for human consumption, and nothing on this site is medical advice.
