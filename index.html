Questions

FAQ

How do I order?

Tap + on anything in the menu to add it to your cart, set quantities, then hit Checkout. Choose shipping or pickup, fill in your info, pay and submit.

Where’s my order?

Tap the button below, enter your order number (or payment code) and email, and tap Send. You’ll get an email back with your order status and tracking link, usually within the hour. Tracking is also emailed to you automatically the moment your order ships.

What’s a COA?

A Certificate of Analysis is a report from an independent lab that confirms what’s in the vial and how pure it is. COAs for tested batches are posted right on the menu (use the Lab tested filter). For anything else, just email me or ask in the Discord. How to read a COA.

Who did the testing?

Some reports are tests I sent in myself. Others come from group tests, where buyers of the same batch pool together and send vials to the lab. Every report says which one it is when you open it, and you can verify each one on the lab’s site.

What’s a blend?

Two or more peptides combined in one vial, like Wolverine (BPC-157 + TB-500). Same compounds, more convenient.

How do I avoid scammers?

I will never DM you first asking for payment, and I never take payment through gift cards, wire transfers or anyone else’s account. Only pay using the details shown at checkout on novrixlabs.shop, with your payment code in the note. If anyone else asks you to pay, on Discord, text or social media, it isn’t Novrix. Email novrixlabs@gmail.com to check.

What does “research use only” mean?

These compounds are sold for laboratory research. They are not approved medications, not for human consumption, and nothing on this site is medical advice.
