Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Short version: we collect what we need to fill and ship your order, we never sell your information, and you can ask us to see, fix or delete it anytime.

1. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, shipping address or pickup time, the items you order, any notes you add, and your research-use confirmation.
  • Payment matching: your payment code, the payment method you chose, the name on the account you paid from, and for crypto the transaction ID. We never see or store card or bank account numbers.
  • Rewards and extras: your Nov Creds balance and referral code, reviews you submit (with the display name you choose), Lab Fund votes and pledges, restock alerts and reorder reminders you sign up for.
  • Messages: emails and Discord messages you send us.

2. What stays on your device

Your cart, language choice, age confirmation, saved checkout details and VIP prices are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them. That information stays on your device and isn't sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

3. How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders, and to confirm payments.
  • To send order updates, tracking, payment confirmations and anything you asked for (restock alerts, reorder reminders, review confirmations).
  • To run Nov Creds, referrals, reviews and the Lab Fund.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep records required by law.

We don't send marketing emails you didn't ask for.

4. Who we share it with

  • Shipping carriers receive your name and address to deliver your package.
  • Service providers that run our email and order forms process your information only to deliver it to us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

5. What's public on the site

Only what you choose to share: the display name and text of an approved review. The order tracker, Nov Creds lookup and shipped-to map use scrambled or combined data, so no names, emails, codes or addresses are readable on the site.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed to support your orders, run rewards and meet tax and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

7. Your choices

Email novrixlabs@gmail.com to see the information we have about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Some records may need to be kept for legal reasons. You can cancel reminders or alerts anytime by replying to the email.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or email system is perfectly secure.

9. Age

Our site and products are only for adults 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always posted here with its effective date.
